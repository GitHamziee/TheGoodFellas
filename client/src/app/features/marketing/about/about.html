About us

We don't buy leads. We generate them.

The Good Fellas (Pvt) Ltd is a Rawalpindi-based lead generation and live transfer operation for insurance clients across the United States.

Who we are

Our own calls, start to finish

We run our own inbound campaigns across Final Expense, Medicare, Health Insurance and Auto Insurance. The people who call us are actively shopping for coverage — they are not a purchased list, and they are never resold to a second buyer.

Every call is screened by AI verification the moment it lands, then confirmed by a live Verifier who checks it against your criteria. Only then is the caller live-transferred to a licensed Closer, who presents the plan, handles the objections and writes the policy while the caller is still on the line.

That is the whole business: generate the call, verify it, transfer it, close it. One accountable pipeline, one status per lead, visible to you the entire way through.
